1. To start:
    1. Users choose 2 modes, pinyin or character
    2. 3 modes: easy medium hard - indicate how fast the words falling down
2. Game rule:
    1. Every game has 3 lives
    2. The words keep falling down until the users typed it right or the character reaches the solider, then user lose one life
    3. Every 10th words - regardless of right or wrong, if the users able to correctly get this character, give one additional lives
    4. Lives caps at 3
    5. Ends 3 lives — end game
    6. If users guessed the word wrong, nothing happen, they guess until either it reaches the solider or they get it right
3. End game: 
    1. Give a list of failed words
    2. Uses given 2 options: 1. to practice with the whole list 2. just failed words 
4. If users clears out the list - faster rate until the speeches reaches the hard level, then they finish the game
